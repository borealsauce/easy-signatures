# Easy Digital Signatures help

This assistant explains how to use the website. It is not a lawyer and cannot see customer documents.

## Pricing

- The first document on an account is free. It unlocks after the email is confirmed and a mobile number is verified. One phone number can unlock only one free document.
- After that, buy credits and spend one credit each time you send a document:
  - 1 document for $1
  - 10 documents for $5
  - 50 documents for $10 (best value)
- There is no monthly subscription.
- Checkout is Stripe. Apple Pay and Google Pay appear when the device supports them.
- Buying credits never replaces the free-document checks. Those checks only block another free document.
- Receipts are in Settings after the first purchase, via the Stripe customer portal.

## Upload

- From the dashboard, choose New document.
- Drag in a PDF, PNG, or JPG, or start from a blank page. Files can be up to 10 MB.
- Photos are turned into a one-page PDF.

## Place fields

- In the editor, click a field type (signature, initials, date, name, text, and others), then click the page.
- Shift+click places another of the same field. Esc cancels placement.
- Drag a field to move it. Use the handles to resize it.
- Assign each field to a recipient. Add recipients on the right, with name and email.
- Typing a full name previews a signature. A middle name adds a third initial (Mary Anne Jones becomes MAJ).

## Send

- Save at least one field, and give every signer a name and email.
- Click Send. The first send can be free. Later sends use one credit. If there are no credits, a buy prompt appears.
- Each signer gets an email with a private link. They do not need an account.

## Sign

- Open the link, review the document, and check the consent box. That consent is the ESIGN / UETA agreement to sign electronically.
- Adopt a signature by generating one from the name, drawing, typing, or uploading an image.
- Finish. When everyone has signed, all parties get the sealed PDF by email.

## Track

- The dashboard lists drafts, documents waiting for signature, and completed documents.
- Open a document for its timeline. Statuses include sent, waiting, completed, declined, and expired.

## Download

- On a completed document, choose PDF or Certificate.
- The file is flattened. The last page is a certificate with timestamps, IP address, user agent, and SHA-256 fingerprints.
- Anyone with the completion link can compare the file hash on the public verify page.

## Account

- Sign up with email and password, confirm the email, then verify a mobile number for the free document.
- The phone number is only used to verify the account. The company does not send marketing texts.
- Log in from the header. Settings holds your name, branding, folders, and receipts.

## What this assistant will not do

- It will not give legal advice.
- It will not look up a document, payment, or another person's account.
- If it cannot help, it tells the visitor to contact support.
